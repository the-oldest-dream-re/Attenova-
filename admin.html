Real Google Sign-In Configuration

Attenova uses Google Identity Services (https://accounts.google.com/gsi/client) and verifies ID tokens on the server with google-auth-library. Set your Google OAuth 2.0 Web Client ID below or via the GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID environment variable on Render.com.

💬 Automated Parent Notifications (Twilio SMS & WhatsApp)

Instantly sends real-time WhatsApp and SMS notifications to parents when their child checks in or when facial/location spoofing is suspected.

Twilio Active
📱 Twilio WhatsApp & SMS Setup Guide:
  • How to receive full Attendance Alerts on WhatsApp: In Twilio Console → Messaging → Try out WhatsApp, find your sandbox keyword (e.g. join brave-tiger) and send it once from your phone to your Twilio WhatsApp number (e.g. +1 415 523 8886). This opens a 24-hour conversation window so custom attendance details (student name, subject, GPS coordinates, maps link, biometric match) deliver directly to your WhatsApp!
  • Automatic SMS Fallback: If the WhatsApp 24-hour window ever expires, Attenova will automatically deliver the attendance alert via SMS instead, so alerts are never lost.
  • SMS Trial Requirement: On Twilio trial accounts, the recipient phone must be listed under Twilio Console → Phone Numbers → Verified Caller IDs, and the SMS From Number must be your assigned Twilio number.
